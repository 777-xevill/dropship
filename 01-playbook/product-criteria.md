# Product Criteria

My validation checklist. I already know how to find products — this file exists so the **decision** is consistent and logged, not so I learn research.

---

## Scoring model

Score each axis 1–5. Anything under the threshold is an automatic no regardless of total.

| # | Axis | What I'm asking | Threshold | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Margin** | Can I hold 60%+ gross margin at a realistic sell price? | ≥ 4 | ×3 |
| 2 | **Wow factor** | Does it stop a scroll in 2 seconds without explanation? | ≥ 4 | ×3 |
| 3 | **Ad-ability** | Can I show the value in video with no talking head? | ≥ 3 | ×3 |
| 4 | **Problem-solving** | Does it fix a real, felt, specific annoyance? | ≥ 3 | ×2 |
| 5 | **Supply reliability** | AutoDS supplier rating, stock depth, backup suppliers | ≥ 3 | ×2 |
| 6 | **Shipping time** | Days to my main market | ≥ 3 | ×2 |
| 7 | **Saturation** | Ad Library volume — too many = late, zero = unproven | ≥ 2 | ×1 |
| 8 | **AOV upside** | Bundles, upsells, multi-pack angle available? | ≥ 2 | ×1 |

**Max score:** 85. **Go threshold:** _(set after 5 tests — start at 55)_

## Automatic disqualifiers

Regardless of score, kill immediately if:

- [ ] Fragile or breaks in transit
- [ ] Requires sizing (returns nightmare)
- [ ] Electrical / battery with certification risk
- [ ] Anything with medical, health, or weight-loss claims
- [ ] Trademarked or obvious IP clone
- [ ] Restricted under Meta ad policy — check before, not after
- [ ] Single supplier, no backup
- [ ] Shipping time beyond my stated promise

## Pre-launch checklist

Before a single taka of ad spend:

- [ ] Scored above threshold on this sheet
- [ ] Unit economics calculated (`unit-economics.md`) — break-even ROAS written down
- [ ] Kill threshold pre-committed in writing
- [ ] 8+ creative variants produced and logged
- [ ] Product page live, tested on mobile, checkout tested with a real order
- [ ] Row created in `03-logs/product-log.md`
- [ ] Offer defined — see `Offer-Lab/` folder

# Hook Library

The first 3 seconds decide whether the ad exists. Everything else is downstream.

Tools change every few months. Hook patterns don't. This file outlives every subscription in `ai-video-stack.md`.

---

## How to fill the swipe file

1. Open **Meta Ad Library** and **TikTok Creative Center**
2. Filter to my niches. Sort for ads running the longest — long run time means it's profitable
3. Pull 40–50 ads
4. For each: transcribe **only the first 3 seconds** and note the visual
5. Tag it with a pattern below
6. Once I have 40+, the patterns that repeat are the ones that work in my niche

Target: **40 entries before I write a single script.**

---

## Pattern taxonomy

### 1. Problem callout
Name the pain in the first line. Viewer self-identifies instantly.
> "If your [X] keeps [annoying thing]..."
> "POV: your [thing] is ruining your [other thing]"

### 2. Curiosity gap
State something incomplete. The brain needs closure.
> "I can't believe nobody talks about this"
> "This is why your [X] never works"

### 3. Before / after
Show the ugly state, then cut. Zero words needed.
> Visual: messy → clean, in under 2 seconds

### 4. Price shock
Anchor high, reveal low.
> "I paid ৳X for this. Then I found this for ৳Y."

### 5. Social proof cold open
Drop into a reaction, not an intro.
> "Okay I was wrong about this"
> "Everyone in the comments was right"

### 6. Demonstration cold open
Just start using it. No preamble. Best for high wow-factor products.
> Visual: the satisfying thing, frame one

### 7. Negative / objection-first
Lead with the doubt the viewer already has.
> "I thought this was a scam"
> "Don't buy this if [X]"

### 8. Us vs them
Contrast the old way against the product.
> Split screen, struggle vs ease

### 9. Specific number
Concrete beats vague.
> "3 things I wish I knew before buying a [X]"

### 10. Interrupt / pattern break
Visual or audio incongruity that stops the thumb.
> Unexpected sound, sudden movement, odd angle

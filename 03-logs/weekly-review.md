## Quarterly checkpoint

Every 12 weeks, answer honestly:

- Is the creative bottleneck actually gone, or did I just buy tools?
- What's my hit rate — products tested vs products that worked?
- Which tool subscriptions earned their cost? Cancel the rest.
- What's in `principles.md` that I'm not actually following?
- Is anything in `02-creative/ai-video-stack.md` outdated? Re-verify tools and prices.

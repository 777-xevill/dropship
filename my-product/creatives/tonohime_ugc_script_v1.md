# TONO HIME — UGC presenter script v1

**Format:** Template B (UGC) adapted to an *explainer*, not a testimonial · 9:16 · ~28 s · 4 clips × ~7 s, each in a different place
**Presenter:** Bangladeshi woman, early 30s, simple salwar kameez + orna, warm "older sister" tone (আপু-style), talking straight to the phone camera
**Tool:** Gemini → Veo (text/image → 8 s clip with speech) · assembly + Bangla captions in CapCut

> **Rule for this ad:** she *explains* the product. She never says "আমি ব্যবহার করেছি" / "I tried it". An AI person claiming personal use is a fake testimonial: it breaks Meta's rules and your "honest brand" positioning. If a real woman films it, she can only say she used it if she actually did.

## Veo prompts (one per clip, generate 2–3 takes each, keep the best)

Use the same "character block" in every prompt so she looks the same across places.

**Character block:**
`A Bangladeshi woman in her early 30s, medium-brown skin, long black hair in a loose braid, small nose stud, wearing a plain teal cotton salwar kameez with a light orna over one shoulder. Natural, minimal makeup. Warm, calm, trustworthy expression, like an older sister giving friendly advice. Filmed vertically (9:16) on a smartphone held at eye level, handheld UGC style, slight natural camera movement, realistic, not glamorous.`

## Quality check per clip (reject if any fail)
- Bangla pronunciation is clear and natural (AI Bangla speech is the weakest link; if it's bad, generate silent clips and record your own female voiceover instead)
- Lips roughly match the speech
- Same face/clothes as the other clips
- Nothing suggestive: no bed in frame, no romantic framing

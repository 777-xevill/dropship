## 0. The short version

1. **Demand is proven, and the market is a price war.** About 15 advertisers are running 50–80 active Meta ads for this kind of product in Bangladesh right now. The leader's ad has run since **2 Aug 2025** (14+ months). Almost all of them copy the same price ladder: **1 = ৳160, 2 = ৳240, 3 = ৳300, plus delivery (৳70 in Dhaka, ৳120 outside)**.
2. **Almost every competitor breaks Meta policy.** They focus on sex and "staying longer", ask "you"-style health questions, and censor words with asterisks (স*হ*বা*স). Meta's policy names that exact trick as banned. They survive by burning through pages and domains. With one new page and your own card, you can't play that game, and you don't need to.
3. **Your edge is to be the opposite brand:** private, honest and comfort-first, for married couples 30+. Nobody is speaking to **40–60 couples (dryness that comes with age)** or to **women**. Meta explicitly allows lube ads when the focus is health/comfort and the audience is 18+.
4. **Don't fight at ৳300.** Compete on the *full price the buyer pays, including delivery*, and on trust:
   - 1 bottle ৳199 + delivery
   - 2 bottles ৳369, free delivery
   - **3 bottles ৳449, free delivery (the main offer)**

   Outside Dhaka, your 2-pack costs the buyer about the same as a competitor's once their delivery charge is added.
5. **Profit depends on one number you don't have yet: your cost per bottle.** At ৳60/bottle you keep about ৳102 per shipped 3-pack before ad costs. At ৳40 you keep about ৳150; at ৳80, about ৳54. See §6.
6. **Targeting:**
   - 1 campaign, 1 ad set, all of Bangladesh
   - **Age 30–65+, with Advantage+ audience OFF** so the age range is a hard limit
   - All genders, no interests
   - 3 ads at launch
   - Budget ৳500 → ৳800 → ৳1,200, raised only while ad cost per order stays under break-even (the most you can spend per order without losing money)
7. **Creatives cost ৳0.** Use phone footage of the gel texture, a wash test and the unmarked parcel, edited in free CapCut with Bangla text. Ten hooks are in §8.
8. **Repeat orders are money competitors never collect.** Their pages die; yours keeps a customer list. Make a re-order offer at day 30–40.
9. **Straight verdict:** by your own `product-criteria.md` this scores **52/85**. It fails the Margin and Wow thresholds and is a Meta-restricted category. If you hadn't bought the stock, I'd say NO. Since you have, run a controlled sell-through and decide on restocking only after 2 weeks of real numbers.

**What this means for your 30–70 target:**
- **Your target is right; just know where the people are.** There are 16.6M users aged 35+. Most of your delivery will go to people aged 30–44. Users aged 55+ number only about 2.5M; they'll still see the ad inside a 30–65+ ad set, so don't build anything separate for them. Meta's highest age setting is "65+".
- **Leaving out 18–29 is your first edge.** People aged 18–24 make up 39% of Bangladeshi Facebook users. Competitors' sex-curiosity ads attract clicks from this group. Your bet is that married buyers aged 30+ place fewer fake orders. That's a **hypothesis**: prove it with your return-rate numbers.
- **Men buy, but women need it more.** 63% of users are men, and every competitor writes for men. There are about 3.9M women aged 35–54 on Facebook, and nobody speaks to them.

### The 40–60 opportunity: dryness that comes with age
- Average age at menopause in Bangladeshi studies: **48.2** (rural Savar) to **51.1** (Kushtia).
- **Vaginal dryness affected 28%** (Savar) to **36%** (Kushtia) of the women studied. 31% reported sexual problems (Kushtia).
- A water-based lubricant is the simplest over-the-counter comfort aid for this. No competitor addresses these couples respectfully. Never claim it *treats* anything; say only that it "helps reduce discomfort".

### How people buy
- **Cash on delivery (COD).** Return-to-origin (RTO) rates are commonly 20–30%, and higher in rural areas. That figure is reported by a vendor (EasySell), so treat it as a rough guide. Keeping your RTO low is a big part of your profit (§9).
- **Embarrassment is the barrier.** Research on condoms found that buyers are more embarrassed about *buying* than about *using* them. The study wasn't in Bangladesh, but the mechanism is the same. Home delivery in an unmarked parcel is half of what you're selling.
- **Prices buyers have already seen:**

## 6. Offer & pricing

### Recommended launch prices
| Pack | Price | Delivery | Buyer's total: you vs. competitor (Dhaka / outside) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 bottle | ৳199 | + ৳70 Dhaka / ৳120 outside | ৳269 / ৳319 vs ৳230 / ৳280 |
| 2 bottles (2 flavors) | ৳369 | **Free** | ৳369 vs ৳310 / ৳360 |
| **3 bottles (3 flavors): main offer** | **৳449** | **Free** | ৳449 vs ৳370 / ৳420 |

Why this shape:
- **Free delivery on 2+ bottles pushes buyers to bigger packs.** One bottle ends up costing ৳269–319 anyway, so the second bottle costs only ৳50–100 more.
- **Outside Dhaka, where most of your buyers will be,** your 2-pack costs about the same as a competitor's and your 3-pack about ৳30 more. Inside Dhaka you're ৳59–79 more. The difference is paid for by trust, privacy and no surprise charges.
- **No fake MRP.** If you want a comparison price, use the real pharmacy price ("ফার্মেসিতে ৫০ মিলি ব্র্যান্ডেড লুব প্রায় ৭০০ টাকা").
- Replace ৳70 / ৳120 with your courier's real rates.

### What you keep per shipped order, before ads
These are **assumptions; replace them with your own numbers:**
- Courier ৳100 on average. You pay it, even when a parcel comes back.
- COD fee 1%.
- Packaging ৳10.
- RTO (returned parcels) 20%. Returned bottles go back into stock.

| Your cost per bottle → | ৳40 | ৳50 | ৳60 | ৳70 | ৳80 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 bottle ৳199 + delivery | ৳95 | ৳87 | ৳79 | ৳71 | ৳63 |
| 2 bottles ৳369, free delivery | ৳118 | ৳102 | ৳86 | ৳70 | ৳54 |
| **3 bottles ৳449, free delivery** | **৳150** | **৳126** | **৳102** | **৳78** | **৳54** |
| *Competitor model: 3 for ৳300 + delivery* | *৳111* | *৳87* | *৳63* | *৳39* | *৳15* |

Each number is **the most you can spend on ads per shipped order, VAT included, and still break even.** Divide it by 1.15 to compare with the "Amount spent" figure in Ads Manager.

The formulas, so you can rerun them:
```
Kept per shipped order = (1 − RTO) × (price + delivery fee collected − bottles × cost − COD fee) − courier − packaging
Break-even ad cost per shipped order (as Ads Manager shows it) = Kept ÷ 1.15
Max cost per conversation = Break-even ad cost × (orders ÷ conversations)
```
Example at ৳60/bottle for the 3-pack: break-even is about **৳88** in Ads Manager. If 1 in 5 conversations becomes an order, you can pay up to **৳17.7** per conversation. If only 1 in 10 does, the limit is **৳8.8**. Your days 1–4 will show which world you're in.

**Two levers matter more than any ad trick:**
- **Cut RTO from 20% to 10%:** +৳26 per 3-pack order at ৳60 cost. Do it with confirmation calls and fraud checks (§9).
- **Buy cheaper:** every ৳10 off your cost per bottle adds ৳24 per 3-pack order.

### Decision rule once your friend replies
- **Cost ≤ ৳45:** healthy. Later you can test 3 for ৳399 to take volume from the price-war sellers.
- **Cost ৳50–65:** use the prices above, push the 3-pack and be strict about returns. Your ad cost per shipped order must stay under about ৳85–100 (VAT included).
- **Cost ≥ ৳70:** the margin is too thin at Facebook prices. Sell the stock with 2- and 3-packs only, and don't restock until you find a cheaper supplier.
- **If buyers in the inbox keep saying "দাম বেশি"** (count how often), drop the 3-pack to ৳399, but only if your cost is ৳50 or less.

## 8. Creative plan: ৳0 production

Your `ai-video-stack.md` lists no paid subscriptions. Keep it that way for this product: free CapCut and Canva cover everything below.

### Shoot list (one 30-minute session with your phone)
1. **Parcel privacy:** a plain sealed pouch or box showing only name, phone and address. Open it to reveal three bottles.
2. **Texture:** squeeze gel between two fingers and stretch it (like `prod2.jpeg`), against a plain dark background.
3. **Wash test:** put gel on the back of a hand, rinse under the tap, and show the skin clean and not sticky.
4. **Bedside:** three bottles on a neat bedside table under a warm lamp. No people.
5. **Scratch code:** only if your bottles have one.
6. **Packing desk:** your hands packing orders, to show it's a real business.
7. **Voiceover:** your own voice, or a female relative's for the women's ads, recorded in a quiet room. Text with soft music also works.

Edit in CapCut (free): vertical 9:16, Bangla text on screen, a cut every ~2 seconds, 15–25 seconds long. Make static ads in Canva (free).

**Don't use:** AI "doctors" or "experts" (like BOND), models, couples in bedrooms, or suggestive fruit shots. Real hands beat AI faces for trust in this category.

### Controlling returns and fake orders
1. **Call every order** before booking the courier. If nobody answers after 2 tries, don't ship.
2. **Check the phone number's delivery history** in your courier's merchant panel before shipping. Steadfast, Pathao and RedX all show a customer's past delivery success.
3. **If the history is bad, or it's a far district ordering 1 bottle,** ask for the delivery charge in advance via bKash or Nagad, or politely decline.
4. **Ship within 24 hours of confirming.** Every extra day raises cancellations.
5. **Track return rate by district** in your sheet. After about 50 orders, exclude the worst areas in the ad set.

### Packaging (what makes "গোপনীয়" true)
- Use an opaque pouch or plain box, with no product name or brand on the outside. Courier item description: "Personal care".
- Tape the bottle caps and add bubble wrap. A leak means a return and a lost customer.
- Insert card: a thank-you note, a patch-test line and a re-order code (§10).

## 12. Numbers to track, kill rules and logging

**Daily sheet (Google Sheets, free):** date · spend (from Ads Manager) · conversations · orders confirmed · bottles per order · shipped · delivered · returned · cancelled before shipping · district.

| Metric | Formula | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Cost per conversation | spend ÷ conversations | Under break-even × order rate (§6) |
| Order rate | orders ÷ conversations | Measure on days 1–4; it's your biggest unknown |
| Bottles per order | bottles ÷ orders | Higher means the price ladder is working |
| Return rate (RTO) | returned ÷ shipped | Your goal: under 15% (a goal, not a forecast) |
| Ad cost per shipped order | spend × 1.15 ÷ shipped | At or under the break-even from §6 |

**Kill rules (commit to these before you spend):**
- **Ad:** once it has spent 3× your break-even ad cost per order (about ৳265 at ৳60/bottle) with zero confirmed orders, pause it.
- **Angle:** if two ads on the same angle are both paused, drop the angle.
- **Product:** after ৳6,000 total spend, if ad cost per shipped order is still more than 30% above break-even and no ad is close, stop. Fix the offer or creative before spending more, or kill it.
- **Scale:** if ad cost per shipped order stays at or below 70% of break-even for 3 days in a row, add 20–30% budget.
